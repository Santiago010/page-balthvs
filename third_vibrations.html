THIRD VIBRATION

Third Vibration is the 3rd album by BALTHVS. It was named honoring an ancient principle of constant movement, everything vibrates, nothing stays still, whatever vibrates higher manifests in a stronger frequency, anything can change of state. All tracks except 'All Is One' were written and produced by Balthazar.

Tracklist:

  2. Komorebi
  3. Ashes
  4. In Plundered Dreams
  5. Famagusta Port
  6. Ojos Verdes
  7. Feelin' Blue
  8. Sun in Gemini
  9. Pajaro de Cumbia
  10. Cosmic Boogie
  11. Pajaro de Cumbia (Nakuma Version)
