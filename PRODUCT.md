# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences share the site, but the design serves the first:

- **Listeners and fans** worldwide, discovering or following BALTHVS. They arrive from Spotify, Instagram, YouTube, a festival lineup, or press. Context: phone in hand, curious, deciding in seconds whether this band is worth a listen. Job to be done: hear the music fast, follow on their platform of choice, find the next show near them, feel they've entered a world that rewards deeper attention.
- **Industry** (promoters, bookers, press, festival programmers) verifying credibility. Job to be done: confirm the touring resume, grab the bio, reach the band.

Primary conversion is **listening** (stream/save), secondary is **following** and **live** (tickets/tour). The mailing list ("SUBSCRIBE") is the owned-audience play.

## Product Purpose

The official home of BALTHVS, a psychedelic trio from Bogotá, Colombia (formed late 2019): 140M+ streams, five studio albums, a live album, twelve EPs, and a global touring circuit spanning 26+ countries and 98+ cities (SXSW, Lollapalooza, Outside Lands, Electric Forest, Montreal Jazz Festival, Estereo Picnic, Rock al Parque; sessions with KEXP, Gilles Peterson, BBC).

The site exists to route every visitor into the music and the live shows while communicating a specific, non-generic identity: psychedelic funk braided with global grooves, Middle Eastern melodies, spiritual-jazz improvisation, and Latin-tinged rhythms. Success looks like: a first-time visitor pressing play and saving a track, a fan finding their city on the tour list, a booker leaving with the bio and a contact.

Members: Balthazar Aguirre (guitar, vocals), Johanna Mercuriana (bass, vocals), Santiago Lizcano (drums, vocals). Catalog anchors: *Transmutations*, *Third Vibration*, *Macrocosm*, *Harvest*, *Cause & Effect*.

## Brand Personality

Mystical, worldly, analog-warm. Three words: **hypnotic, borderless, alchemical**.

Voice is understated and confident, never hype. The music is trippy but the presentation is composed: it invites the visitor into a world rather than shouting for attention. The album vocabulary is hermetic and cosmic (transmutation, vibration, macrocosm, cause and effect) and that mysticism is the connective tissue. Emotionally the site should feel like the lights going down before a set: warm, intimate, a little otherworldly, drawing you inward.

## Anti-references

- **Latin-band cliché.** No tropical-postcard palettes, no palm-and-neon "Latin music" signaling. The band is on record defying Latin stereotypes; the site must too. Global, not exotic.
- **Neon-on-black psychedelia.** The EDM/crypto default for "psychedelic" (electric purple/cyan on pure black, laser gradients) is the training-data reflex. Their psychedelia is analog and earthy, not rave.
- **Spotify-for-Artists template sites** and generic link-in-bio landing builders that look interchangeable across ten thousand acts.
- **Over-polished major-label pop gloss.** No sterile hero-video-plus-CTA corporate feel. This is an independent, craft-forward act.
- **Gradient-text and glassmorphism decoration** used to seem "modern."

## Design Principles

1. **Music is the destination.** Every surface is one tap from hearing them. Copy and chrome never stand between the visitor and play.
2. **Build a world, not a page.** The mystical/alchemical through-line (album names, imagery, the ritual of the lights going down) governs mood. Atmosphere over information density.
3. **Analog warmth over digital gloss.** Vinyl, film grain, earthy warm-dark tones, texture. Intimate, tactile, hand-made feeling, not flat-design slick.
4. **Show the resume, don't boast it.** The touring and press credibility (SXSW, Lollapalooza, KEXP, 26 countries) is proof of seriousness; present it plainly and let scale speak. No superlative padding.
5. **Borderless by design.** Signal global reach and cross-cultural blend without leaning on any single-culture visual shorthand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Warm-dark theme demands verified text contrast (AA for body, large-text AA minimum for display over imagery/backgrounds). Provide `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks for any atmospheric motion (autoplay/hover/scroll effects), and keep embedded players (Spotify, YouTube) keyboard-reachable with meaningful labels. Alt text on album art, logo, and band imagery. Mobile-first: most fans arrive on a phone.
