HARVEST

Harvest is the 4th album by BALTHVS. Balthazar Aguirre, Johanna Mercuriana, and Santiago Lizcano. It is the first BALTHVS album that was composed by all three band members. Recorded in La Mesa, Colombia, it reflects the tropical environment in which it was created and the abundance for which it was manifested.

Tracklist:

  1. Sun Colored Eyes
  2. Asha
  3. Anouk
  4. Like Coconut Water
  5. Lovin'
  6. Aguacero
  7. Mango Season
  8. Sun & Moon
  9. Venus Flytrap
  10. Aio
Recorded in La Mesa, Colombia between August and September 2023
Mixed and Produced by Balthazar Aguirre & Santiago Lizcano
Artwork by Balthazar Aguirre
