• ABOUT •

It's not far fetched to consider BALTHVS as the biggest psychedelic band Colombia has ever produced. Formed in late 2019, with over 140 million streams, five studio albums, 1 live album and twelve EPs, the band has established itself in the global touring circuit, performing across the Americas, Europe, Asia, and Oceania.

They have played more than 26 countries and 98 cities worldwide, appearing at major events including SXSW, Lollapalooza, Austin City Limits, Outside Lands, Electric Forest, Lightning in a Bottle, Montreal Jazz Festival, SXSW Sydney, and Jazz Cafe Festival. The band has also made notable appearances on KEXP, Gilles Peterson’s Worldwide Cafe, and the BBC.

BALTHVS continues to push forward with a distinctive blend of psychedelic funk, global grooves, Middle Eastern melodies, spiritual jazz improvisations, and Latin-tinged rhythms

BALTHVS is:

Balthazar Aguirre (Guitar, Vocals)

Johanna Mercuriana (Bass, Vocals)

Santiago Lizcano (Drums, Vocals)

Psychedelic, Adj:

From Ancient Greek ψυχή (psukhḗ, “mind, soul”) + δῆλος (dêlos, “manifest, visible”) + -ic.

Coined by English psychiatrist Humphry Osmond in 1956 in a letter to Aldous Huxley.

"BALTHVS: An Evolving Concept"

The Psychedelic Experience is clearly present but it has mutated into another form: Psychedelic is external, it doesn't come from within, watching a kaleidoscopic 4d film inside your mind, such experiences leave lessons and temporary states of transcendence. Far too many don't go beyond this temporary chemical high, some become forever lost in the trip.

"All Music is Psychedelic"

It generates trance and guides emotions: Harmony, Melody and Rhythm create mood, they change vibration. Rarely does it divide men from one another, more than anything it unites.

"Imagination Realizes What it Invents"

We are only left with the power of imagination and will to transmute that which lies inside, burdensome thoughts, into alchemical reality. To transmute the psychedelic experience into the Cosmic, in the realm of the Manifest. Our pretensions are only to alleviate the daily toll the mind, the body and spirit take amidst our modern, uncertain world, if only a little bit. Music is medicine. Music is an Escape.

If there's just a tiny less bit of anger or anxiety because of this work, the task is done, the purpose is fullfilled, there is no other intention beyond this, it is a means to an end.
